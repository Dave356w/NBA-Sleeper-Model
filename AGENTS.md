# Repository guidance

This is a new independent Sleeper-only model. Follow MODEL.md, including the owner’s ESPN fetch-timestamp override, the prospective v1.20 residual-defense update, the v1.21 shrinkage and reporting update, the v1.23 from-game-1 residual-defense update, the v1.24 season-history confidence calibration, the v1.25 applied team offense, the v1.26 game-day first snapshot, the v1.27 priced first snapshot with closing-price backfill the v1.28 latest-snapshot fit history, the v1.29 late-lock fallback, the v1.30 game-day replay, the v1.31 game-day team fit, the v1.32 240-minute cap and the v1.33 win-probability scale centre. Do not edit any other repository. Keep raw scoring, fixed controls, stages, provenance and all accepted records immutable. Never add an external scoring feature or retroactively change a threshold. All methodology changes are prospective and versioned. From v1.22 only the moneyline creates selections (`selection_markets`); spread and total quotes are stored as context. From v1.27 only the directional (model-pick) series registers (`selection_series: [directional]`); the value series is retired, and forecasts frozen without the key keep every series. The public season record pools replayed earlier games with live results at render time (`season.py`). By owner update of 11 November 2026 Results and Performance show only that pooled current-version record, without sample-size or replay labels, live-only tables or the fixed control; the pages do not state the live and replayed counts (owner decision), How it works describes the replay, and every part stays in `data.json` and the ledger. The Today page's live scores and live win chance are browser display only; `web/live_wp.json` is an owner-authorized copy of nba-matchups' display coefficients and must never feed scoring, selections, grades or training; replay never writes a forecast, selection or grade; from v1.24 its walk-forward margins start the confidence-calibration history.

Before changing logic, run the relevant fixture tests. Before publishing, run `ruff check src tests`, `ruff format --check src tests`, `pytest -q`, `python -m sleeper_nba validate`, and `python -m sleeper_nba build-site`. Tests must not depend on live APIs. Browser smoke tests are optional after UI changes and use a locally served generated site. Do not put synthetic fixtures in the native transaction ledger.

The authoritative data is data/transactions. Preserve accepted forecasts on rendering failures; derived exports may be regenerated. Never print tokens or credentials. ESPN odds require no external API secret.

The owner authorized incorporating residual defense with 25-calendar-day decay and testing shrinkage on last season. Through v1.24 the scoring component is defense-only with a 20-game offensive nuisance control; from v1.25 (owner update, 19 November 2026, `matchup.apply_offense`) each team's fitted offense term is applied to its own score and the defensive prior is 20, while fits recorded under earlier versions reproduce with defense only (see `research/team_terms_replay.py`). v1.20 used a 5-game defensive prior; the owner-requested v1.21 review raised it to 40 (see MODEL.md and `research/defense_prior_replay.py`). From v1.28 (`matchup.history_snapshot: latest`) a game with native first snapshots under several tags trains the team fit on its latest one, as the season replay does; configurations without the key keep ledger order. Each methodology version keeps its own team-residual and confidence histories, and public pages show the current version only. First-stage parameter history includes the explicitly labeled, hash-pinned 117-game legacy-first seed. From v1.23 (owner update, 10 November 2026) the residual-defense correction applies from the first graded game of a season, locks are scored with the first-stage team fit (seed included; no lock-only team fit), and the team fit refreshes once each slate is fully graded; forecasts frozen earlier keep the 30-game minimum, lock-only history and daily refit. Through v1.23 confidence calibration is stage-specific and never uses the seed. From v1.24 (owner update, 11 November 2026) the first-stage confidence history starts from the season replay (`calibration.history: season`, seed included, walk-forward) plus native games of the current tag, and locks use the first-stage calibration (`calibration.stage_source`); the minimum, weighting, shrinkage and daily cap are unchanged, and configurations frozen without these keys keep the earlier behavior. Preserve raw/fixed forecasts and v1.19/v1.20 histories. By owner update of 17 November 2026 the games missed in the 14–17 November capture outage are backfilled from nba-matchups' archived early-day Sleeper snapshots through the hash-pinned `matchup.backfill_files` (team fit from its creation, season replay, replay calibration history); they never become forecasts, selections, grades or ledger records. Probability calibration starts a separate history for each version and uses frozen corrected targets, with no extra scalar scoring biases. From v1.26 (owner update, 19 November 2026, `first_policy: game_day_et_v1`) the first snapshot is the earliest valid capture on the game's Eastern date, and from v1.27 (`game_day_priced_et_v1`) the earliest such capture with a qualifying DraftKings moneyline, unpriced from T−5h if none. From v1.27 the season record prices games whose frozen snapshot had no price at the DraftKings close from the hash-pinned `closing_backfill` file (ESPN game summaries); it is reporting only, never replaces a snapshot price and never creates forecasts, selections, grades or ledger records; forecasts frozen without `first_policy` keep the earliest valid capture. From v1.29 (`lock_policy: five_hours_late_v3`) a lock is on time in the 15-minute window after T−5h and otherwise a labelled late lock (`lock_timing: late`) for the first valid capture until `lock_late_hours` (2) before tip; locks frozen under `five_hours_15min_v2` keep the narrow window. The watcher's pre-capture tests are non-blocking and the watchdog alerts on a stalled capture. Earlier locks used the 15-minute window after T−5h (`five_hours_15min_v2`); forecasts frozen under the v1.20.1 through-tip policy keep it. Capture runs from the self-chaining `watch.yml` job with `watchdog.yml` as its restart; do not add a second writer or a cron that shares the writer concurrency group. From v1.30 (`replay_snapshot: game_day_et_v1`) the season replay, and through it the confidence history, uses only snapshots captured on the game's Eastern date: a game-day native lock replaces an earlier-day first snapshot, and a game with no game-day capture is excluded and listed (`season.replay_excluded`); configurations frozen without the key keep the earlier replay. From v1.31 (`matchup.history_snapshot: game_day_latest`) the team fit follows the same game-day rule; fits recorded under `latest` or without the key keep their histories. From v1.32 (`matchup.team_minutes_cap: 240`) the adaptive scoring, the team fit and the season replay scale a team projected above 240 minutes down to 240 (never up); raw scores and the fixed control are unchanged, seed and backfill minutes come from the hash-pinned `matchup.seed_minutes` file, and configurations without the key score uncapped. Latest previews are untracked and must never create bets, grades or training observations.

From v1.33 (owner update, 20 November 2026, `wp_initial: 13.3`, `wp_prior_centre: 13.3`, `models.adaptive_defaults`) the adaptive win-probability scale starts at 13.3 and is shrunk toward 13.3 instead of 10; weights, prior strength, minimum, bounds and the daily cap are unchanged, the fixed control keeps a = 10, and configurations without the key keep 10.
